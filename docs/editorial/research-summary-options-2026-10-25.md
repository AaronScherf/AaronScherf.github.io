## Recommended framing

The current copy foregrounds a general concern about certainty and only demonstrates one project. The revised agenda supplies a more memorable subject: how people withstand overlapping crises, and which forms of support help them recover. A reader can understand that subject before encountering a method name.

The homepage already introduces the USAID background, offers specific consulting services in How I Can Help, and displays Selected Projects before My Research. This block can therefore lead with the research question, then connect it to a small number of demonstrated methods. Repeating the full biography or service list would use space better devoted to the future agenda.

Use resilience, response, and recovery in ordinary prose without requiring the 3R acronym. Translate anticipatory action as assistance before a crisis escalates, data fusion as combining records, and heterogeneous effects as differences across communities. The faculty-oriented version can name causal inference as an intended method, while past-tense skill claims stay anchored in the actual portfolio. The public-facing drafts omit faculty names, anticipated partnerships, speculative findings, and delivery dates.
